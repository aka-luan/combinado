# Prior knowledge: session cookies

The learner already understands session cookies as the mechanism that keeps someone logged in across requests. Future lessons should treat “session” as known ground and teach OTP as the short-lived proof that *creates* a session, not as a replacement for it.

## Implications

- First lesson = OTP vs session (bridge), not “what is a cookie”
- Avoid cookie-lifecycle deep dives unless a misconception appears
